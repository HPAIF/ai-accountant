> how the founder is paid, how a recurring transaction is treated, accepting a tax risk.

> - **Options considered:** …
> - **Chosen:** … · **Decided by:** founder
> - **Why:** …
> - **Based on:** regulation cards / memos (with verification dates)
> - **Review when:** event or date that should trigger a re-check
> ```
