# Source registry — Armenia (tax & accounting)

> Which sources count, and how to use them. Tier decides what a source can prove.
> If a URL here stops working, find the new one on the official site, use it, and record a
> `source` learning so the pack gets fixed upstream. URLs marked *(confirm)* have not been
> confirmed yet; open and confirm them on first use.

## Tier 1 — the law itself (can prove what the law says)
| Source | URL | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| ARLIS — Armenian Legal Information System | https://www.arlis.am | Official consolidated texts of laws, government decisions, ministerial orders, with edition dates and amending acts. **Primary source for every rule.** Armenian text is authoritative. |
| National Assembly | https://www.parliament.am | Bills (draft amendments to the Tax Code etc.), adopted laws before ARLIS publishes them, entry-into-force dates. |
| e-draft — unified site for draft legal acts | https://www.e-draft.am | Government drafts in public discussion (often Tax Code / accounting changes months before adoption). Comment deadlines. |
| Constitutional Court | https://www.concourt.am | Decisions declaring tax provisions (un)constitutional. |
| DataLex — judicial information system | https://datalex.am | Court decisions: Administrative Court and Court of Cassation tax disputes (Cassation interpretations bind lower courts). Use the non-`www` host. |
| EAEU legal portal | https://docs.eaeunion.org | VAT/excise rules for trade with EAEU members (imports of goods/services from Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan). |

## Tier 2 — regulators (prove how the law is applied; official guidance, forms, deadlines)
| Source | URL | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| State Revenue Committee (ՊԵԿ / SRC) | https://www.src.am | Official clarifications (պարզաբանումներ), forms and instructions, tax calendar, cash register (ՀԴՄ) rules, e-invoicing, news of changes, penalty practice. |
| SRC e-filing system | https://file-online.taxservice.am *(confirm)* | Where returns are submitted (the founder submits; the AI never logs in). Form versions. |
| SRC e-invoicing system | https://e-invoice.taxservice.am *(confirm)* | Electronic tax invoices: formats, instructions. |
| SRC tax news portal | https://www.petekamutner.am *(confirm it is SRC's)* | SRC publications and explanations. |
| Ministry of Finance | https://www.minfin.am | Accounting and audit policy, adopted IFRS / IFRS for SMEs texts, accounting regulations. |
| Central Bank of Armenia (CBA) | https://www.cba.am | Official exchange rates, currency regulation, payment/e-money rules (only if money passes through the company). |
| Ministry of High-Tech Industry | https://hightech.gov.am | IT-sector support programs, grants, incentives and their conditions. |
| State Register of Legal Entities | https://www.e-register.am | Registration, company extracts, state duties for registration. |

## Tier 3 — professional interpretation (leads + reasoning; never the basis alone)
- Big-4 / large firms' Armenian tax briefs and guides (PwC, EY, KPMG, Grant Thornton, Deloitte).
  Useful for "what changed this year" summaries.
- Association of Accountants and Auditors of Armenia (AAAA), ACCA Armenia — professional notes.
- IFRS Foundation (https://www.ifrs.org) — standard texts and interpretations (framework only;
  which version applies in RA comes from Tier 1/2).
- Double tax treaties: text on ARLIS (Tier 1); OECD commentary as interpretation.

## Tier 4 — leads only
- News: news.am, arka.am, banks.am, investor.am, armenpress.am, etc.
- Telegram channels and forums of Armenian accountants, Facebook groups.
- AI summaries (including your own past memos — re-verify).

## Rules of use
1. A conclusion must rest on Tier 1 (what the law says), ideally with Tier 2 (how SRC applies it).
2. Tier 3–4 can tell you *where* to look and *what* changed — then confirm on Tier 1/2.
3. If Tier 1 and Tier 2 conflict, say so: the law prevails legally; SRC practice shows audit risk.
4. Record every source used in the answer's **Verification** block and, for lasting topics, in a
   regulation card.
5. Never send company documents or facts to any of these sites. Search for the rule only.
